⚠️ Product Safety & Care Information
  • Choking Hazard: All items—including beaded pens, keychains, bag charms, phone wristlets, earrings, and wedding tokens—contain small components (silicone/acrylic beads, metal spacers, rhinestone rings, and rubber charms) that may pose a serious choking hazard.
  • Not a Toy: Our products are strictly intended for adult use, styling, and functional accessory purposes. They are not toys and are completely unsuitable for infants, toddlers, or small children. Please keep them safely out of reach.
  • Pet Safety: Keep these items away from household pets. Chewing on cords, charms, or beads can cause injury or accidental ingestion.
  • Jewelry & Resin Care: To prolong the life of your sterling silver, freshwater pearls, and hand-poured resin pieces, avoid direct contact with water, heavy perfume, lotions, and harsh cleaning chemicals.
