Store Policies

Thank you for supporting our UK handmade boutique studio! By purchasing from our website, you agree to the terms, delivery times, and product disclaimers outlined below.

📦 1. Delivery & Processing Times

  • Processing Time: All of our items are handcrafted with care in our UK studio. Orders are processed and dispatched within 1 to 3 business days.
  • Shipping Method: We ship orders securely across the UK using Evri or Royal Mail.
  • Standard Shipping Fee: £3.65 - £4.99 dependant on size of parcel.
  • Estimated Transit Time: Once dispatched, orders typically arrive within 2 to 4 business days.

Please note: Sparkling Resin Delights is not responsible for any courier delays once your package has been handed over to Evri, but we will always assist you in tracking your parcel.

🔄 2. Returns, Refunds & Exchanges

In accordance with the UK Consumer Rights Act, we want you to be completely happy with your purchase:

  • Reporting a Fault: If your item arrives broken or faulty upon delivery, please contact us within 14 days of receipt with a photograph of the damage. We will happily arrange a replacement or a full refund.
  • Change of Mind Returns: Due to the small-batch, handmade nature of our items, buyers are responsible for return postage costs if they wish to return an item due to a change of mind. The item must be returned in its original, unused condition within 14 days of delivery.
  • Exclusions: Custom or personalized orders cannot be returned or refunded unless they arrive damaged.

⚠️ 3. Product Safety Disclaimer & Limitation of Liability

  • Intended Use: All items sold by Sparkling Resin Delights (including charms, pens, keychains, ornaments, and jewelry) are handcrafted boutique accessories intended solely for decorative or personal wear.
  • Safety Warning: Our products contain small components, including acrylic beads, natural stones, metal charms, and small hardware fittings, which may pose a choking hazard. Our items are not toys. They are not intended for infants, children under 3 years of age, or any individual who may ingest small objects.
  • Junior Collection Notice: Items inside our Junior Collection are designed for older children as fashion accessories. Adult supervision is strictly required at all times while these items are being handled or worn.
  • Limitation of Liability: Upon successful delivery of the item to the buyer, Sparkling Resin Delights accepts no responsibility or liability for any subsequent wear and tear, accidental damage, alteration, misuse, or handling of the product. By completing your purchase, you acknowledge that proper use and supervision of the item is the sole responsibility of the buyer.

⚠️ 4. Product Safety & Care Information

  • Choking Hazard: All items—including beaded pens, keychains, bag charms, phone wristlets, earrings, and wedding tokens—contain small components (silicone/acrylic beads, metal spacers, rhinestone rings, and rubber charms) that may pose a serious choking hazard.
  • Not a Toy: Our products are strictly intended for adult use, styling, and functional accessory purposes. They are not toys and are completely unsuitable for infants, toddlers, or small children. Please keep them safely out of reach.
  • Pet Safety: Keep these items away from household pets. Chewing on cords, charms, or beads can cause injury or accidental ingestion.
  • Jewelry & Resin Care: To prolong the life of your sterling silver, freshwater pearls, and hand-poured resin pieces, avoid direct contact with water, heavy perfume, lotions, and harsh cleaning chemicals.
