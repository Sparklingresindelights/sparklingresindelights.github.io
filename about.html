Our Story

Welcome to Sparkling Resin Delights!

Hello! We are Dee and Sophie, a mother-daughter duo based in the UK, handcrafting boutique keepsakes and treasures from our home studio. Our little business started as a shared passion, but it quickly became an incredibly important sanctuary for both of us.

I live with Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) and battle severe chronic pain daily. Finding a way to focus my mind outside of the pain is a daily challenge, and crafting has truly become my escape. It gives me a beautiful sense of focus, peace, and purpose on the hardest days.

My daughter, Sophie, lives with ADHD, which brings its own unique set of daily challenges. Together, we found that sitting at our workbenches, working with vibrant colors, textures, and designs, is where we both feel the happiest and most grounded. We absolutely love what we do, and doing it together makes it even more special.

Who Makes What?

We work as a team, each pouring our energy into the separate crafts we love most:

💖 Made by Dee

I focus on creating our hand-beaded bracelets, custom keychains, luxury beaded pens, and personalized Christmas tree decorations to bring sparkle to your holiday traditions.

✨ Made by Sophie

Sophie is the creative mind behind our intricate resin jewelry collection, our hand-decorated notebook bundles, as well as her own beautiful custom pens and keychains.

Every single item you see in our shop has been lovingly designed, matched, and assembled by our own hands. When you buy from us, you aren't just receiving a gift—you are helping to support our wellness, our family, and our handmade dreams.

Thank you so much for stopping by and being a part of our journey!
